Privacy Policy

CoinhakoFin ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the CoinhakoFin mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CoinhakoFin is designed from the ground up to keep your data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone, address)
  • Financial data (accounts, transactions, balances, holdings)
  • Receipts, images, or OCR-extracted content
  • Authentication credentials (because no account is required)
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or tracking identifiers
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or behavioral data
  • Location data or contacts

2. Data Storage

All financial records, transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, investments, receipts, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using local, sandboxed storage. The App requires no internet connection to function and performs no network requests related to your data.

3. On-Device OCR

Receipt scanning uses on-device optical character recognition (OCR). Captured images and extracted text (merchant, amount, date) are processed locally and stored only on your device. No image or extracted text is uploaded to any server.

4. Backups & Exports

You may export your data as CSV or JSON files, or restore from a backup you previously created. These files live on your device or in your personal iCloud / Files storage at your discretion. We never see, transmit, or have access to your exported data.

5. Third-Party Services

CoinhakoFin integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or social networking services. There are no SDKs that collect user data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children, as we collect no information at all.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Because we collect no data, any changes will pertain only to clarifying or strengthening our privacy commitments.

8. Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control. You may export, edit, or delete any or all of your data at any time directly within the App.

Common Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your data lives exclusively on your device in a secure, sandboxed location. It is never uploaded to our servers (we have none for your data), and it is never shared with third parties.

Does CoinhakoFin work offline?

Yes. The App is fully functional without an internet connection. The currency converter uses pre-loaded exchange rate data bundled with the App.

Can I export or back up my data?

Yes. You can export your complete records as CSV or JSON files at any time, and restore from a backup with one tap. You can also save backups to iCloud or your Files app at your discretion.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual records inside the App, or remove everything at once by deleting the App from your device. There is no remote account to deactivate because no account is created.
